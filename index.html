What is 8000Hz HyperPolling and does my computer support it?

8000Hz polling sends 8,000 motion updates per second to your CPU (0.125ms delay). It is recommended for monitors running at 240Hz, 360Hz, or higher paired with modern Intel Core i7/i9 or AMD Ryzen 7/9 processors.
